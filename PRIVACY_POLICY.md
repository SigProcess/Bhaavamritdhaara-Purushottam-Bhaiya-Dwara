# Privacy Policy

**Bhaavamrit**  
*Last updated: May 17, 2026*

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Bhaavamrit mobile application ("App") handles user information. Bhaavamrit is a devotional music (bhajan) playlist app for Anand Pranami bhajans by Purushottam Bhaiya.

The App is a Trusted Web Activity (TWA) that displays content from our website (purushottambhaiya.netlify.app) within a full-screen browser experience on your Android device.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or process any personal data.** The App does not have:

- User accounts or login systems
- Analytics or tracking SDKs
- Advertising frameworks
- Forms or data entry fields
- Access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or files

## Permissions

The App requests the following permission:

- **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** Used solely to deliver push notifications about new bhajan playlists or updates. You can opt out of notifications at any time through your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

The App displays content that relies on the following third-party services:

### YouTube (Google)
The App embeds YouTube playlists and videos for playing devotional music. When you watch a video, YouTube's own privacy policy and terms of service apply. YouTube may collect data such as viewing history and device information in accordance with their policies.

- YouTube Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

### Google Chrome / Trusted Web Activity
The App uses Google Chrome's Trusted Web Activity feature to display web content. Chrome may collect standard browsing data as described in Google's privacy policy.

### Netlify
Our web content is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, access times) as part of normal web hosting operations.

- Netlify Privacy Policy: https://www.netlify.com/privacy/

## Data Storage

The App does not store any personal data on your device or on any server. Any browsing data (such as cached content) is managed by Google Chrome and subject to Chrome's data handling practices.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal data from any user, it is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
